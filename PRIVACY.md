# Privacy Policy — Drive Mirror

Drive Mirror is a personal tool used only by its owner.

- It accesses only the owner's own Google Drive, to upload copies of the owner's own files.
- It does not collect, store, sell, or share any data with anyone.
- No data leaves the owner's computer except to the owner's own Google Drive.
- Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
